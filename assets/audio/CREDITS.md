Original MP3 bytes are embedded unchanged as Base64 in piano-samples.js; each entry includes its exact source URL and SHA-256. Playback adjusts volume and fades/cuts tails; playback rate stays 1 (no pitch shifting). The repository's MIT tooling license is not being used as the sample license. Samples are bundled locally; gameplay does not contact the source site.

scale-preview.mp3 is a listening preview assembled from these same eight notes, with volume adjustment, shortened/faded tails and pauses between notes. It uses the same attribution/license as the samples. pitch-verification.json records measured fundamentals, not a claim of physical-speaker listening.
